Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Short version: This game does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Your high score is saved only on your device.

1. App Overview

Data Cleaner: Outlier Invaders is a retro arcade mobile game developed for entertainment purposes. The app is free to play and contains no advertisements and no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information, including but not limited to: name, email address, device ID, location, or usage data.

The only data stored is your local high score, which is saved exclusively on your device using the browser's localStorage mechanism. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

3. Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting tools, or social media integrations. No data is shared with any third party.

4. Internet Permission

The app requests INTERNET permission as part of the standard Capacitor WebView runtime. The game itself runs entirely offline once installed — no network requests are made during gameplay.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13, and we do not direct our app at children. Since no data is collected at all, COPPA compliance is maintained by design.

6. Data Safety (Google Play)

For the Google Play Data Safety section, please declare:

  • Data collected: None
  • Data shared with third parties: None
  • Data encrypted in transit: N/A
  • Users can request data deletion: N/A (no data stored server-side)
